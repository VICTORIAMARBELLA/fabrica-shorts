Privacy Policy

Fabrica Shorts · Last updated: November 20, 2026

Fabrica Shorts ("the tool") is a private, internal application operated by the owners of the YouTube channels Six vs Five and Sixperiments. It is not offered to the public. Only the channel owners sign in to it.

1. Data we access

With the channel owner's explicit consent through Google OAuth, the tool uses these YouTube permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload Shorts that the owners have created and approved to their own channels.
  • youtube.readonly: to read basic information about the owners' own channels and videos (titles, IDs, publishing status).
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read the owners' own channel statistics (views, retention, engagement) to improve future videos.

The tool does not access any account other than the owners' own channels, and it does not access viewers' personal data.

2. How we use it

Data is used only to publish our own videos and to analyze their performance. We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Storage and security

OAuth tokens and statistics are stored only on devices and services controlled by the channel owners, protected with access restrictions. They are never published or shared.

4. Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept only as long as needed to improve our content. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions; after revocation the tool can no longer access the account, and stored tokens are deleted.

5. Google API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Fabrica Shorts' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
